Privacy Notice

Last updated: May 2026

Emkari respects your privacy. This Privacy Notice explains what information we collect when you place an order, contact us, or interact with our website, and how we use that information.

Information We Collect

When you submit an order or contact form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, order details, pickup or delivery preference, delivery street address, delivery ZIP code, payment method preference, and any notes you choose to provide.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process cookie orders, send order updates, confirm pickup or delivery details, estimate delivery fees, respond to messages, and provide customer support.

Text Message Updates

If you agree to receive SMS updates, we may text you about your order, including confirmation, pickup, delivery, payment, or other order-related details. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Payment Information

We do not collect credit card or bank account information through this website. If you choose a payment method such as Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, or cash, payment details may be handled through those services or confirmed directly with you.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only when needed to operate our business, process orders, communicate with you, comply with legal requirements, or use trusted services that help us manage orders and messages.

Delivery Information

If you request delivery, we use your delivery street address and ZIP code to estimate or confirm delivery availability and cost. Delivery fees shown on the website are estimates and may be confirmed by text before your order is finalized.

Data Retention

We keep order and contact information only as long as reasonably needed for order records, customer service, business operations, or legal requirements.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide. However, no website, form, or online service can guarantee complete security.
